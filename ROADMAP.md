# Roadmap

## Near term

- Add more generic product families and installed-view comparison fixtures.
- Improve supplier questions for forming, tooling, finish, and assembly tradeoffs.
- Add repeatable second-pass search checklists for preferred concepts.

## Later

- Add jurisdiction-specific source adapters where official public records are available.
- Add structured sample-review acceptance criteria for design teams and suppliers.

## Non-goals

- Providing legal advice, FTO clearance, or infringement opinions.
- Hiding client designs, unpublished applications, or attorney work product in a public package.
