# Design-right screening and design-around

`design-patent-search-and-design-around` · English

## Purpose and scope

Search appearance rights, explain complete drawings and develop structurally distinct redesigns for professional review.

## Implementation Basis and Dependencies

Identifiable product, sufficient multi-view visuals, jurisdiction and cutoff. Current primary patent/design-office records are required for current conclusions. Visual search and image generation are optional.

## Inputs

Product URL/model, perspective/top/side/end views, jurisdiction/date; optional brand, assignee, factory, known rights and notices. Redesign requires function, material, tooling, fitment and cost constraints.

## Outputs

Search log, candidate register, drawing-scope explanation, view comparison, separate risk conclusions, three redesign directions, feasibility gates, second-pass search and attorney handoff.

## Workflow

1. Normalize identity and scope.
2. Search images, terms, classes, entities, families and citations independently.
3. Verify complete primary drawings/status/ownership.
4. Compare overall appearance and solid/broken lines.
5. Separate substantive, complaint, ownership and unpublished risks.
6. Change several salient structural relationships, test feasibility and re-search the preferred concept.

## Boundaries and stopping conditions

No formal FTO or guarantee of non-infringement. Color or one feature change is not automatic clearance. Missing critical views or authoritative records limits conclusions. Generated concepts are not engineering drawings; outreach and filings require permission.

## Example request

Use `$design-patent-search-and-design-around` — Pre-screen a mount using all material views; propose three distinct architectures and supplier questions, then re-search the preferred direction; respond in English and preserve evidence, unknowns and action boundaries.
