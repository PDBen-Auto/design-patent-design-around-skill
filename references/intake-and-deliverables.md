# Intake And Deliverables

## Minimum Intake

```text
产品链接 / ASIN / SKU / 型号：
产品名称与用途：
目标国家或地区：
产品图片：主图、安装图、顶视图、侧视图、正/后视图、端视图、细节图
品牌 / 卖家 / 法律实体：
供应商 / 实际工厂 / 设计人：
已知专利号、注册设计号或投诉信息：
必须保留的功能和结构：
允许修改的部分：
材料、工艺、承重、成本、安装和包装限制：
期望输出：检索结论 / 完整报告 / 三套规避方案 / 多视图 / 标注图 / 供应商清单
报告语言与格式：
计划上市时间：
```

If only a marketplace URL is available, extract the available identity and image evidence, list missing views, and lower confidence for hidden geometry. If the user provides a private supplier report or complaint notice, keep it out of any public artifact unless the user explicitly authorizes disclosure.

## Standard Output Contract

### 1. Executive conclusion

State the product, jurisdiction, cutoff date, confidence, and what the research does not establish.

### 2. Evidence register

List source URL, source type, retrieval date, relevant fact, and whether it is fact, inference, or assumption.

### 3. Candidate register

Use one row per serious candidate:

```text
Candidate ID | publication/grant | title | article | applicant/assignee | inventor/designer | priority | status | relevant drawings | source links | verification state
```

### 4. View comparison

At minimum compare perspective, top, side, front/rear, end, and installed relationship when available. Explain missing views instead of filling them by inference.

### 5. Risk register

Separate substantive infringement, marketplace complaint, ownership, unpublished application, prior-art, and operational reversion risk.

### 6. Design-around package

Provide three distinct directions, a salience matrix, functional and manufacturing trade-offs, supplier questions, and the preferred concept's non-reversion list.

### 7. Attorney handoff

List unresolved records, formal FTO questions, claim/drawing sets to verify, and the evidence a qualified attorney should review.

## Supplier Evidence Request

Ask the supplier, as appropriate, for:

- actual factory and industrial designer identities;
- all relevant CN/US/Hague or other jurisdiction application and registration numbers;
- priority documents, assignments, and licenses;
- earliest disclosure and sale evidence;
- mold and tooling ownership;
- written non-infringement and indemnity terms;
- cooperation terms for marketplace complaints;
- evidence that the production drawing matches the reviewed design.

## Public-Release Redaction

Before publishing a case or report, remove real customer names, private supplier contacts, credentials, unpublished application details, confidential pricing, and raw marketplace account identifiers. Replace them with synthetic labels and clearly mark the example as illustrative.
