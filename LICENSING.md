# Licensing and edition policy

This repository currently uses a source-available license. The public edition makes the generic pre-screening workflow, references, synthetic examples, tests, and release provenance inspectable without publishing client legal work.

## Public edition

The package may be used only within the rights granted by `LICENSE`. It is intended for generic product-development pre-screening and evidence organization.

## Reserved material

The following remain outside the public package:

- client product drawings, unpublished applications, attorney work product, and confidential supplier records;
- private research data, credentials, and deployment configuration;
- commercial legal review, FTO work, and custom design-risk services.

## Official release status

Forks are not official releases unless their public manifest and publisher provenance signature verify against the published key. This document does not turn the Skill into legal advice or grant rights beyond `LICENSE`.
