# Security Policy

## Supported surface

Report issues in public-record retrieval guidance, drawing comparison, evidence packaging, design-around planning, or release-boundary checks.

This Skill is a product-development pre-screen. It is not legal advice, an infringement opinion, or FTO clearance.

## Reporting

Use GitHub private vulnerability reporting when available. Otherwise contact the repository owner privately. Do not publish confidential designs, unpublished applications, supplier identities, attorney work product, or client files.

## Operational rules

- Reproduce issues with the generic or synthetic examples in the repository.
- Remove credentials, private URLs, personal data, and confidential product drawings from reports.
- Treat official records and source links as evidence; do not turn a similarity score into a legal conclusion.
- Do not claim that a modified fork is an official publisher release.
